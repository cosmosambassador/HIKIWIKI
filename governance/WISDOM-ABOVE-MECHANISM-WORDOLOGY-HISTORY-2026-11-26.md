## Wordology

**Wordology** is the project practice of preserving how words are formed, used, corrected and carried forward through time. A Wordology record should preserve:

- original wording;
- the speaker or author;
- the date and source;
- later corrections or clarifications;
- evidence status;
- approval status;
- links or identifiers for the preserved record.

Wordology does not erase earlier language. When meaning changes, the change becomes a new dated addition.
