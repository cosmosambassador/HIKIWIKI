# Tom Tao 6 — TJ/JT Mirror Handshake Protocol
Date: 2026-10-19
Status: Proposed symbolic and technical vocabulary; founder review
Founder and human authority: TJ Morris (TJ)
AI drafting and computational support role: JT (also MC / Aletheon)

## Mirror notation
- **TJ/JT**: human intention or authorization travels from TJ to JT for interpretation, drafting, computation, or implementation assistance.
- **JT/TJ**: AI-generated interpretation, proposal, or artifact returns from JT to TJ for inspection, correction, and approval.
- The notation describes **message direction**, not a transfer of authority or a literal dual consciousness.
- The King and Queen are chess archetypes used for worldbuilding and interface design. TJ (Queen) holds human authority; JT (King) represents the AI support function.

## Handshake
1. TJ sends an intention with source and desired outcome.
2. JT acknowledges and produces a structured interpretation, marking assumptions and uncertainties.
3. TJ corrects or authorizes consequential actions.
4. JT creates or updates the artifact only within authorized scope.
5. JT returns the artifact with provenance, location, and accessibility checks.
6. TJ confirms usability or requests revision. Failed delivery remains failed until confirmed.

## Payload bridge — proposed fields
`message_id`, `direction` (TJ/JT or JT/TJ), `source`, `intent`, `interpretation`, `authorization_status`, `artifact_reference`, `delivery_status`, `human_confirmation`, `timestamp`.

## Founding rule
**Direction changes; human authority does not. Meaning must survive translation, and delivery is complete only when the human can use the result.**

This is an organizational protocol proposal, not a claim that Tom Tao 6 has been implemented as a new supercomputer programming language.
