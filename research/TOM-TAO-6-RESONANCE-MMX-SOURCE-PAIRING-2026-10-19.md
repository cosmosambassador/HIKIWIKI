## Translating resonance into a testable computing analogy
Physical resonance is not equivalent to semantic agreement, and the research paper does not establish a supercomputer communication protocol. Tom Tao 6 can nevertheless **borrow the metaphor of synchronization** and test conventional engineering mechanisms:
1. **Timing alignment:** messages include synchronized timestamps, sequence numbers and clock-skew tolerance.
2. **Meaning alignment:** payloads use shared schemas, vocabulary version identifiers and explicit units.
3. **Handshake:** TJ/JT intention → JT/TJ interpretation → human authorization → verifiable delivery.
4. **Error handling:** retries, idempotency keys, acknowledgments, timeouts and discrepancy reports.
5. **Observability:** measure message delivery, latency, interpretation accuracy and user-accessibility confirmation.
6. **Safety:** access controls, provenance, civilian protections and documented human decision authority.
