This is the shared working dictionary and chronology for the ACO, AISI, ACE, ACIR, Aletheon, Aeonic Codex, Cosmos Expanse, HIKIWIKI, World Information Network, Eight Earth Plates, and collaborating AI systems.

Wordology preserves the power and history of language without pretending that words alone establish external facts. A new term may create a useful category, role, story, or operating rule. Scientific, historical, legal, and technical claims still require evidence appropriate to their domain.

# Part XII — Wordology Rules for the New Era

1. Define a new term before building policy around it.
2. Preserve exact original wording where historically important.
3. Identify the person, model, provider, or institution responsible for a term.
4. Record the date, source, and version.
5. Separate standards, provider language, project terms, canon, testimony, and interpretation.
6. Never turn a role name into a claim of autonomous deployment.
7. Never turn a belief or symbolic narrative into a scientific conclusion without evidence.
8. Do not infer religion, politics, or other sensitive traits about a person.
9. Represent internal diversity within religions, cultures, political movements, and communities.
10. Use primary sources for current political procedures, laws, model policies, and technical standards.
11. Keep public pages readable by people and machine systems.
12. Name one canonical public page and label other copies as mirrors.
13. Reduce unnecessary duplication without deleting historical provenance.
14. Make corrections through dated additions and version history.
15. Keep consequential action under human authority.
16. Let technology serve memory, dignity, evidence, education, and community.
