## Publication and correction policy

1. Preserve the original source, author, date, and status.
2. Separate established standards from project-created terminology.
3. Separate testimony and interpretation from independently verified fact.
4. Use dated additions for corrections; do not conceal earlier wording.
5. Label public mirrors and identify their source records when known.
6. Do not claim deployment, certification, endorsement, or institutional authority without evidence.
7. Keep private credentials, recovery information, confidential sources, and internal access maps out of the public repository.
